Fetches your Grimmory libraries into the local grimmory.db, then moves wishlist books you own from the lists to the Purchased tab and refreshes the BookBub deals owned flags. Also runs automatically on the 1st of each month. Needs the GRIMMORY_USERNAME / GRIMMORY_PASSWORD settings configured; a run takes a few minutes and runs in the background — the bar and activity window above follow it live, and it keeps going if you leave the page.

The BookBub time runs the whole daily cycle at one time (the same time for all): fetch the day's BookBub deals, update the owned-in-grimmory flags, remove duplicates, and re-verify every non-expired deal against live Amazon prices — through the NordVPN tunnel, which is only connected while that run is in progress. The cover-size and tooltip-size defaults also drive the matching dropdowns on the BookBub Deals tab.
